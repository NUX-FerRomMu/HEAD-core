# HEAD — Human-Engaged Agentic Development

**Use your HEAD.**

HEAD is a customizable agentic development environment designed to put humans in control of AI-driven software development.

Build your own workflow, orchestrate coding agents, track their progress, review their work, and decide what happens next — all within an environment tailored to the way you develop.

HEAD embraces a simple philosophy: **AI agents do the work, but you do the thinking.**

Your agents. Your workflow. Your HEAD.
